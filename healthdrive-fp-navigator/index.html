HealthDrive Family Planning Navigator

A counselling pathway on HealthDrive's Unstructured Supplementary Service Data (USSD) service, which works on any basic phone with no internet. Dial the short code, answer a few questions, and get a short answer checked against the World Health Organization (WHO) medical eligibility criteria for contraceptive use. Anything that needs a person goes to a community health worker.

Try it: press Dial, then use the keypad. Menu names stay discreet ("Wellness") and the service keeps no readable history on the phone.
