About

I'm a Nigerian founder and builder based in Kigali. I hold a BSc in Software Engineering (First Class Honours) from African Leadership University and a BSc in Life Sciences (Cum Laude) from the University of Ilorin. I lead the data science and machine learning work behind a rural health platform running on USSD over 2G telephony, and I co-founded Clisense, a climate-smart agriculture venture built around the Water-Energy-Food nexus.

My research work spans low-resource speech processing, health informatics, and implementation science. I published a peer-reviewed paper in the International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology, and I completed the Carnegie Mellon University Africa Bridge Program, a selective preparatory track for graduate study in engineering and technology.
