About

Researcher and builder.

A little about the path, the training, and what I'm focused on today.

I'm a researcher. I hold a degree in Software Engineering (First Class Honours) from African Leadership University and a degree in Life Sciences (Cum Laude) from the University of Ilorin. My work combines machine learning, health infrastructure, and low-resource language technology. I build and study systems that hold up under real constraints: limited connectivity, scarce training data.

My research work spans low-resource speech processing, health informatics, and implementation science. I published a peer-reviewed paper in the International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology, and I completed the Carnegie Mellon University Africa Bridge Program, a selective preparatory track for graduate study in engineering and technology. In March 2026, I received the Most Entrepreneurial Researcher Award at the 2nd Primary Health Care Congress, hosted by Amref International University in Nairobi.
